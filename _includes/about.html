Hello 👋!

I completed my PhD in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon University in September 2026, where I was advised by Jean Oh (Bot Intelligence Group) and Jonathan Francis (Robot Learning Lab).

My research focuses on enabling autonomous navigation systems to seamlessly and safely interact with humans in shared spaces. During my PhD, I developed algorithms and methods to assess and enhance the robustness of motion prediction models and social navigation algorithms.

Most of my work has focused on aviation and autonomous driving, but I have also explored other domains such as motion in human crowds and sports.
